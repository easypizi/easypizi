## 🔭 HireScope

I built [HireScope](https://hirescope.tech), AI-powered company research for job seekers. Paste a company name or job URL (optionally attach a resume) and get a structured report.

- Company passport, funding, tech stack, and risk signals
- Culture insights and compensation benchmarks
- Resume match and interview prep

**Try it:** [hirescope.tech](https://hirescope.tech)
